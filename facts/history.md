## People and community evidence

The FOSDEM deck identifies **Stéphane Magnenat** as presenter and explicitly credits community contributors through the distribution AUTHORS file. [Swiss Games Showcase](https://zarkonnen.github.io/SwissGamesShowcase/) attributes original work to Magnenat and Luc-Olivier de Charrière; that latter attribution is secondary evidence pending direct creator/archive corroboration. SourceForge lists **genixpro** and **k776** and its Beta 1 news credits Bradley’s core/YOG work. Snap publicly displays **Bradley Arsenault (genixpro)**. Legacy wiki news names **ProgVal** in the 2012/2017 hosting transitions. These records document roles at those times, without assigning current ownership or exclusive authorship.

## Historical assets and coverage leads

The primary FOSDEM slide deck and [legacy video index](https://globulation2.org/wiki/Videos) preserve historical explanations and match footage. Wiki screenshot pages preserve older visual generations, including alternate artwork. [Linux Journal issue127, November 2004](https://files.serialport.org/media/Linux_Journal/Linux%20Journal%20issues%201-131%20PDFs/Linux%20Journal%20-%20127.pdf) is a discovered period coverage lead describing indirect tasks and a map editor; full bibliographic/rights review remains open. Wiki manuals, forum maps/campaigns, translated material and Savannah/SourceForge archives are relevant knowledge and community assets, not just obsolete downloads.

## Unresolved history

- Exact project founding day, earliest public pre-alpha, original contributor roles and the predecessor’s public distribution record.
- Complete 2009–2026 release chronology, including 0.9.4.4/0.9.5 histories and when each source host became authoritative.
- Formal continuity of domain, artwork, contributor permissions, community accounts and present release administrators.
- Scope and start date of the modern revamp; tags establish dated artifacts, not a complete narrative of intent.
- Reliable historical player/download totals. Package inclusion, forum accounts, SourceForge counters and GitHub stars are different quantities and have not been combined into an audience estimate.

No inactivity period or causal explanation is inferred from gaps in the consulted sources.
