Globulation 2

OverviewAbout the game

Build a thriving colony
in contested territory.

Food, workers and space shape your expansion. Grow a sprawling colony, balance its competing needs, and contend with rivals for land and resources. Your creatures act autonomously, letting you guide the colony as a whole.

GENRES

Strategy · Real-Time Strategy

FOCUS

Colony building · Resource management

FOOD. WORKERS. GROWTH.

Grow an economy that
can sustain your ambitions.

Food, construction and training compete for your workforce. Decide where workers are needed and balance new ambitions with the needs of the colony you already have.

ROOM TO GROW

Make the map
part of your strategy.

Shape your base around the land and resources. Choose where to expand, and consider what each new foothold means for your economy and your rivals.

THE COLONY AS A WHOLE

Guide the colony
as a whole.

Set worker demand and place flags while your creatures handle their own movement and needs. Guide the colony through its priorities, and adapt as its needs change. Your economy supports both growth and defense as rivals compete for land and resources.
