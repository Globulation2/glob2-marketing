# Storefront-v2 key art

[Manifest](manifest.json) · [Build script](build-assets.py) · [Concepts](concepts/) · [Derivatives](derivatives/) · [Plates](plates/) · [Desktop capture tooling](../captures/desktop/README.md)

Built 2026-11-24 to replace `assets/epic-v5/offer-landscape-2560x1440.jpg`, an AI-painted landscape (natively 1672×941, upscaled) that does not look like the game. Everything here is made from **real in-engine captures of Globulation 2 0.11.7.0 + 15 commits (revision 5bf966285aa8), the installed build; maps, AI roster and simulation identical to the v0.11.7.0 tag** (not the public 0.11.7.0 release binary; see [claims R13](../../../facts/storefront-claims.md)), plus design treatment. No AI-generated or stock imagery, and no painting.

```sh
uv run --with pillow python build-assets.py            # everything + manifest.json
uv run --with pillow python build-assets.py --only derivatives
```

The script is deterministic: two runs on this machine gave byte-identical files. It checks every output's pixel size, format, file-size limit and wordmark clearance, and writes `manifest.json` with inputs, outputs, SHA-256, crop boxes and scale factors.

### Recommendation: A, Shoreline colony

- **It reads in one second.** At 460×215 and even at 231×87 you see buildings and many small creatures on an island by the sea: a lively top-down colony game. The red globs stand out against green grass. The lime globs in B nearly vanish on grass, and C's sand and stone read as terrain rather than a colony.
- **It has a calm place for the wordmark.** The water L gives every crop from 3:1 to 2:3 a dark, calm area. The cream wordmark sits on real fogged water, with a soft night halo instead of a heavy scrim. In B the wordmark covers part of the colony. In C it covers the bottom channel, and a bottom-placed title is easy to lose under store UI.
- **The plate is clean.** It has no HUD, no health or food bars, no flag circles, no zone outlines and no selection. B's plate still shows small unit and building bars.
- **It holds up beside the comparables.** It sits in the same family as Factorio and Mindustry: real game art, a dense scene, a large logo. It lacks the painted depth of Against the Storm and Timberborn, which is expected for a top-down game.
- **Its weakness is that it is peaceful.** The only rival on screen is one cyan explorer in the forest; the red warriors along the shore are not fighting. C is the only concept with a visible fight. If the reviewer weighs action over readability, C is the alternative, and its derivatives can be generated by changing `RECOMMENDED` and the derivative specs.

## Derivatives (concept A)

All in `derivatives/`. Each one is re-framed for its aspect from the 4396×2600 plate, not centre-cropped. Every one is a downscale (scale factor ≤ 0.963) of native pixels. Wide frames keep the colony left and the wordmark on the water L. ~2:1 frames use a tighter crop so the swarm and schools read at 460 px. Tall and square frames shift left to hold the swarm, barracks and pool, and run the wordmark across a darkened top band over the fogged fields and the sea. The wordmark is the game's title art: never wider than 1.15× its native 1941 px, and always at least 3% clear of every edge.

## Treatment applied

All of it is in `compose()` in `build-assets.py`, using Pillow only:

1. **Crop and scale down** one rectangle of the plate (Lanczos). The script refuses any upscale.
2. **Grade:** contrast ×1.05, saturation ×1.08.
3. **Depth of field:** a Gaussian blur (about 0.16% of the output width) blended in toward the edges, away from a focus point on the colony. This is a lens effect: nothing is moved or repainted.
4. **Scrim:** a soft night-violet darkening behind the wordmark, an edge vignette and, in tall frames, a top band. It is the picture multiplied by a violet tint and blended toward night `#1b1229`. Masks are dithered with seeded noise, so there is no banding.
5. **Wordmark:** `glob2/content/data/gfx/menu-wordmark.png` (the game's title art, 2103×748), un-mixed into letter and "2" masks and set in cream `#f9e8bb` and gold `#e2b85f`. It has a night glow, a short drop shadow and a thin night outline. The transparent logos use the outline and shadow only.

No game object is added, removed, moved or redrawn. The wordmark is the only text.

## Plates and provenance

Each plate is one rectangle, x 0–4396 and y 440–3040, of a single native 4806×3040 frame. That rectangle is left of the sidebar and below the top bar and the event list. It was cut losslessly; nothing was scaled or retouched.

Capture conditions, common to both new plates:

- **Build:** Globulation 2 0.11.7.0 + 15 commits (revision 5bf966285aa8), the installed build; maps, AI roster and simulation identical to the v0.11.7.0 tag. Not the public 0.11.7.0 release binary ([claims R13](../../../facts/storefront-claims.md); `glob2 info version` reports version 0.11.7.0, revision 5bf966285aa8). `/Applications/Glob2.app`, executable SHA-256 `252fd7d7ff8383c2f72760eccb418b4792bfdfe9f820cd8925b8d8cb69cb7c7f`.
- **Platform:** macOS on Apple silicon.
- **Window:** off-screen with SDL's dummy driver, `--renderer portable-sdl --window-size 4806x3040 --ui-scale 2`, launched by `captures/desktop/tools/launch-capture-game.sh`. HD artwork was forced on, and the log shows no step-down.
- **Settings:** clouds and cloud shadows off. Health and food bars were switched off with the `I` key, which also hides war-flag range circles (they are drawn only with that lens).
- **Zoom:** 150% (96 px a tile), a seam-free step.
- **Timing:** the match was running at normal speed (1×) at the moment of capture. It was not paused, so the frame has no "Paused" label.
- **Seat:** a local player joined to one colony of a headless all-AI save with `tools/join-seat.py`. That colony's AI keeps playing it.
- **Orders:** the seat's only orders were camera moves and erasing its own colony's forbidden, clearing and guard markings inside the view. These are the outlines the AIs paint around buildings, and a player can erase them from the Flags and areas tab. Each frame was checked for leftover outline pixels and retaken when the AI had repainted them.
- **Spectator view:** these are AI-built colonies seen from a player seat. In footage-rule terms they are AI versus AI, not human play.

## Known weaknesses

- **No fight in the recommended image.** The shoreline plate shows a busy colony, warriors along the shore and one rival explorer, but no combat. In these AI matches, large fights next to a home cluster are rare (see the battle scans in `captures/desktop/README.md`). Concept C carries the action, but without a colony.
- **The water L is mirrored from the brief.** The water runs along the top and right, not the top and left: the FourSquares island corner with the densest colony faces that way, and the art is not flipped. So the wordmark sits top right in wide frames.
- **The plate is 4396×2600**, not 5120×2880, because the capture window was the 4806×3040 already in use. Every derivative is still a downscale.
- **The forest is busy.** The violet forest is a large patterned area at the right of the wide frames. It is softened by the depth-of-field blur but still competes a little at full size.
- **Portrait frames use a top band, not water.** In the 2:3 and 3:4 frames, the left half of the band behind the wordmark is darkened colony and fields.
- **Small effect sprites stay in.** A few small real game sprites, such as a green "+" heal effect and a yellow glob effect, remain in the frame.
- **File sizes.** About 36 MB of plates, 59 MB of derivatives and 11 MB of concepts. The Git steward may want LFS. Nothing is committed.
