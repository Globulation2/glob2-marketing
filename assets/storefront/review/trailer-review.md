### Store gates (not file fixes)

- **Google Play.** Every cut with English, Japanese or Arabic text carries "No ads. No in-app purchases." The claims record says the Play Console ads and in-app purchase declarations are **not recorded** ([claims §6](../../../facts/storefront-claims.md#6-store-declarations)). Read both in the console before the YouTube URL goes on the listing.
- **AI co-play.** The desktop variant must stay off Epic and the Mac App Store until claims R8 and R7 clear. It also stays off itch.io: one embedded video cannot be scoped to the desktop downloads on a page that also offers browser play.

## What was checked and how

- **Frames.** For every deliverable, ffmpeg extracted a frame every 0.25 s for 0–3 s and every 0.5 s after that, plus the last frame: 66 frames for a 30 s cut, 72 for the desktop cut and 36 for the 15 s cut. Each set was viewed as a phone-feed strip (360 px wide landscape, 200 px wide portrait), with full 1920×1080 or 1080×1920 frames for every shot and every text card. The 2160p files were spot-checked at 0.0, 4.5 and 22.5 s, with a 100 % crop of the 4K text. Closer crops were made of the placement (2.5–4.9 s at 5 fps), the Inn completion (5.8–7.3 s), the clash (0–2.5 s and 15.0–18.5 s at 4 fps), the co-play sites (26.0–29.5 s at 2 fps) and the Hive card at 4K.
- **Opening.** Frame 0 is live gameplay with warriors already in contact. There is no black, logo or fade, so Epic's non-black cover frame is satisfied. The melee is real: in the 4 fps crops, red and lime units interpenetrate and trade places through 0–2.5 s and 15–18.5 s.
- **Each line against its footage.** "You decide. They build." runs over the Inn placed by the seat (4.1 s) and completed by workers (about 6.5 s). True, but not readable (M2). "Keep the colony fed." runs over wheat, harvesters and inns. True. "Fight rivals for the land." runs over the red and lime melee. True. "No ads. No in-app purchases." is a product claim, true of every client per the claims record. "Play alongside your AI agent." is true of the take but not shown (M7). There is no "free", price, call to action, store badge, URL, "first" or online claim in any cut.
- **Footage rule.** The landscape masters have no fog-black, menu or early-game frames. Fog of war dims, but does not blacken, the top of shot 4 and the top right of shot 7. The weakest landscape shots are 5 and 7 (S2, S3). The verticals' shots 5 and 7 fail the rule (M4, M5). The manifest labels the footage as AI-versus-AI spectator footage, filmed from a seat.
- **HUD crop.** The HUD is cropped out of every shot except the co-play card. This is acceptable for the brand master, the mobile listings and social, because the map view is the same on every platform and the cards carry the story. It is not acceptable where a line depends on the interface (M2). Steam encourages a visible HUD, and the footage rule asks for the actual interface, so the M2 fix should put the real build palette on screen at least once.
- **Typography.** Brand cream on a dark scrim; the English cap height is about 60 px at 1080p and stays legible in a 360 px thumbnail. The Japanese line 1 is the longest at about 76 % of the width. The Arabic shaping, joining and right-to-left order are correct.
- **Technical.** All files are H.264 High, yuv420p, constant 30 fps, AAC stereo 48 kHz, with durations of 30.0, 33.5 and 15.0 s. Sizes are 30–158 MB: every 1080p file is under Epic's 300 MB, and every file is under Microsoft's 2 GB. The posters are 1920×1080 and 3840×2160 RGB PNGs without alpha.
- **Audio** was measured with `volumedetect`, `ebur128` and 0.5 s RMS windows. Nobody listened to it.
- **Hive card in the release.** Release tag `v0.11.7.0` contains the card (`src/hive/HiveDialog.cpp`, "Stop commander" in `content/data/texts.en.txt`). Local standing-order cards are created in `EngineRun.cpp` without the Commander feature gate, so the card is real release interface. This was read from source only; no build was run.
