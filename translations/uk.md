# Globulation 2 storefront translations — uk

Source version: storefront-v2. Observation date: 2026-11-24. Locale: uk.

Provenance: AI translation with independent AI linguistic review; not human or native-speaker validation. Translated afresh from the storefront-v2 [English master](en.md) following the [field map](storefront-v2-field-map.md) and [terminology](glossary.md); `epic_tail` and `epic_legal` keep their storefront-v1 text. Current review status is held in the hash-bound review record, not in this preamble. Terminology notes: the creature name (глобули) and the names of buildings, flags, resources, landscapes, replays and online modes follow the game's own Ukrainian text catalog. "In-app purchases" is rendered as «покупки в грі» in every field, because the Ukrainian App Store and Google Play label in-app purchases with different words for "app" and this store-neutral gaming term is also the only one that fits the overlay and trailer limits. `play_short_description` drops "offline" to fit 80 characters, as the field map's cut order prescribes. `ios_keywords` is a search-term adaptation, not a literal list: it carries "offline" both as «офлайн» and as «без інтернету», the phrase Ukrainian players commonly type for offline games. "MCP server" is written «MCP-сервер» throughout. The independent review of 2026-11-24 revised wording in the keyword, map, multiplayer, AI-agent, itch.io and caption fields.

## ai_body

У Globulation 2 є вбудований MCP-сервер. Увімкніть його в налаштуваннях, і ШІ-агент на вашому комп’ютері зможе бачити гру та грати за ту саму колонію разом із вами. Ваші миша й клавіатура весь час працюють, тож роботу можна ділити як завгодно: доручіть агентові постачання їжі, а самі плануйте атаку, або просто спостерігайте, що він робить.
