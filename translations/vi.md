# Globulation 2 storefront translations — vi

Source version: storefront-v2. Observation date: 2026-11-24. Locale: vi.

Provenance: AI translation from the [English master](en.md), following the [field map](storefront-v2-field-map.md) and [terminology](glossary.md), with independent AI linguistic review. This is not human or native-speaker validation. `epic_tail` and `epic_legal` keep their reviewed storefront-v1 text. Game terminology reference: `content/data/texts.vi.txt` in the Globulation2/glob2 repository (thuộc địa, công nhân, chiến binh, quán trọ, tổ, đường chạy, hồ bơi, doanh trại, tháp phòng thủ, tường đá, cờ chiến đấu, kiểu địa hình, bản ghi trận đấu, trình biên tập bản đồ, đấu nhanh, xếp hạng, giao hữu, thiết lập).

Translator notes: "globs" is kept as the name "glob" (Vietnamese has no plural ending). "Computer opponent" is "đối thủ máy tính"; "AI agent" is "tác nhân AI". In-app purchases use Google Play's term "mua hàng trong ứng dụng" in running text and the App Store's shorter "mua in-app" in the 28- and 32-character image and trailer fields. `shot_overlay_hero` and `ios_subtitle` keep the full genre term and leave out "colony", which does not fit. `play_short_description` drops "offline" to keep the genre, "no ads" and the colony/unit contrast within 80 characters. `shot_overlay_fair_deal` and `trailer_line_4` state the same two facts with the same terms; only the connective differs ("&" at 28 characters, "hay" at 32). `windows_search_term_7` uses "AI agent" in Latin letters because that is the form people type into a search box, and `windows_search_term_5` and `ios_keywords` use "chiến thuật" and "offline", the common search words for the genre and for "ngoại tuyến". Reviewer notes: screen orientation in `android_tail` and `ios_tail` is "màn hình dọc" / "màn hình ngang", the words players use for portrait and landscape, kept apart from "kiểu địa hình" (the game's 67 random landscapes). "Premium currency" is "tiền tệ trả phí" in both `fair_deal_line` and `shot_caption_fair_deal`.
