# Launch positioning v1

[Analysis index](README.md) · [Task ledger](../planning/tasks.md) · [Earlier options](positioning-options.md) · [Product facts](../facts/product.md)

Selected by the user **2026-10-26** through discussion. This is the agreed working positioning for launch, open to later revision. It is a user decision, not a player-research finding or a claim that the wording has been validated with an audience. No live copy or listings have been changed.

## Agreed copy

**Build a thriving colony in contested territory.**

Globulation 2 is a real-time strategy game where food, workers and space shape your expansion. Grow a sprawling colony, balance its competing needs, and contend with rivals for land and resources. Your creatures act autonomously, letting you guide the colony as a whole.

## Message emphasis

- Lead with colony building and the interdependent economy: food, workers, growth and competing needs.
- Make spatial strategy part of that experience: where the colony grows and how it uses land and resources.
- Rival colonies and warfare supply pressure; battles are part of the game rather than the whole promise.
- Autonomous creatures and distinctive controls support managing the colony as a whole. Control innovation, Hive Mind and JavaScript tooling are not the launch's lead promise.

The [product ledger](../facts/product.md#gameplay-and-player-surfaces), especially P01–P03, supports indirect control, economic needs and rival-match objectives. The user emphasized the importance of large bases, spatial strategy and the colony economy in choosing this wording. No quantitative scale, comparative superiority or audience-demand claim has been established.

## Decision and next use

The user stopped the recruitment/testing approach and chose to develop a sufficient launch v1 through conversation. The [full validation plan](validation-plan.md), [three-player kit](../planning/three-player-test-kit.md) and [recruitment draft](../planning/three-player-recruitment.md) remain historical preparation; their execution is not an active launch prerequisite. No outreach or player sessions occurred.

Use the agreed copy as the reference for future launch-material work. Canonical imagery, channel choices, commercial model and publication remain separate work; this decision does not itself change live surfaces or authorize outreach.
