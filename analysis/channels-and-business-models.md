# Channel and business-model possibility map

[Analysis index](README.md) · [Audiences](audiences.md) · [Jobs to be done](jobs-to-be-done.md) · [Comparables](comparables.md) · [Facts](../facts/README.md)

Prepared **2026-10-26**. All options, audience responses, benefits, risks and proposed research below are **analyst hypotheses**, unless explicitly identified as observed evidence. Ordering is thematic, not a ranking. No channel, price, funding model or audience is selected. No outreach, advertisement, sale, account creation or public change has been executed.

## Evidence base and commercial boundary

The [channel ledger](../facts/channels.md) establishes a modern website/app, a linked but unverified Discord invitation, GitHub and historical community surfaces. The [distribution ledger](../facts/distribution.md) separates live downloads and Snap from older packages and unverified store submissions. The [asset ledger](../facts/assets.md) records multiple screenshot/copy families. None supplies acquisition, retention, community activity or willingness-to-pay evidence.

The [product ledger](../facts/product.md) records free/open-source gameplay and source implementations for skins, separate AI-service credit wallets, optional creator services and reporting. These systems are not verified live offers or validated business models. Current source docs describe Hive Mind prepaid credits without subscription, expiry or automatic top-up; a subscription below is a new possibility, not an existing entitlement.

The GNU project's [GPL FAQ](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-faq.en.html#DoesTheGPLAllowMoney) describes charging for GPL software while preserving recipients' redistribution freedoms. That permits examining paid distribution as a possibility; it does not establish rights to every bundled artwork, track, article or trademark, nor settle a particular store's terms. The [asset provenance gaps](../facts/assets.md#sound-authored-content-and-licensing) remain material. No alternative proprietary license or exclusive relicensing authority is assumed.

## Interactions and tensions to investigate

- **Player and payer can differ:** a browser player, AI creator, club organizer, donor and teacher may use different capabilities. One persona does not imply one commercial model; see [audiences](audiences.md) and [jobs](jobs-to-be-done.md).
- **One message versus several entrances:** colony autonomy, competitive RTS, creative coding, open-source history and AI-assisted creation could explain distinct experiences. A broad pitch could obscure first play; a narrow pitch could hide relevant capabilities. Neither tradeoff is resolved.
- **Community size versus segmentation:** platform-native convenience could add players, while versions, paid content, regions, rule variants and private rooms could divide matchmaking. No actual concurrency or cross-version compatibility measurements support an outcome.
- **Core fairness versus assistance:** cosmetic expression, authored content and AI commands have different possible effects on competition. Source support for AI in ranked does not establish player acceptance or competitive neutrality.
- **Funding versus trust:** free/open-source identity, legacy no-ads/no-IAP copy, optional web payments and possible AI services require precise scope. Trust effects of each framing need direct research; no contradiction is resolved by this analysis.
- **Generative tools versus authored craft:** creator assistance may reduce some effort but require more review and rights disclosure. Output validation, artistic quality, enjoyment and player willingness to use AI are distinct questions.
- **Global reach versus operational capacity:** more languages, stores and communities can mean more support and moderation surfaces. Listing count is not a measure of successful launch coverage.
- **Recurring revenue versus recurring promises:** subscriptions, managed hosting and memberships imply continuing obligations; one-time products also require clear support/version expectations. Source payment plumbing alone answers neither sustainability nor margin.

## Questions and measurements that could separate possibilities

These are candidate research questions rather than approved next steps. No numerical threshold or priority is selected.

| Question | Candidate evidence | Interpretive limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Which explanation predicts satisfying play? | Observe people with contrasting motivations attempt the same tutorial/first-colony tasks after different accurate descriptions | Small samples reveal friction, not population shares or market size |
| Which channel adds returning players? | Source-aware cohorts from first meaningful action to later voluntary return, with support effort recorded | Attribution gaps and selection effects remain; followers, downloads and visits are not interchangeable |
| Who pays for what outcome? | Interviews about actual prior purchases, followed if authorized by a clearly scoped offer with actual price | Stated willingness and real payment differ; no deceptive checkout/fake availability |
| Can a model cover its obligations? | Net receipts after platform/payment/refund costs versus compute, hosting, moderation, support and production labor | Gross revenue or provider estimates alone do not establish profit; observed periods may be unrepresentative |
| Are optional AI services useful and acceptable? | Successful outcomes per attempted task, time/cost to usable output, opt-out behavior, fairness perceptions | Technical validation does not prove fun, learning or competitive equality |
| Do creator systems bring players? | Artifact completion/publication, independent use, resulting play and maker return | A library item count does not establish demand, quality or a marketplace |
| Can old communities reconnect? | Consent-based conversations and dated activity checks at existing surfaces | Nostalgia and goodwill need not mean regular play or purchases |
| Does cross-platform expansion help? | Qualified installation/controls/network tests and comparison of return/support by device | A build pipeline or store listing is not physical-device quality |

The present evidence does not establish channel efficiency, market size, sustainable price, buyer demand or a preferred combination. These remain open possibilities for later user-directed validation and decisions.
