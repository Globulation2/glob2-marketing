# Analysis: marketing possibilities

[Repository overview](../README.md) · [Task ledger](../planning/tasks.md) · [Facts](../facts/README.md)

This section explores hypotheses and alternatives, separate from the factual estate inventory. The [launch positioning v1](https://github.com/Globulation2/glob2-marketing/blob/4e62f805935fa57724d66338071ee8553f059f18/analysis/positioning-v1.md) records the user’s subsequent message decision, and [positioning v2](positioning.md) the decision that replaced it on 2026-11-24; neither represents validated market demand.

## Possibility maps

- [Channels and business models](channels-and-business-models.md): candidate acquisition/engagement channels, current mechanisms and new offers, tradeoffs and possible distinguishing evidence.

- [Comparable games](comparables.md): twelve historical/current reference cases, bounded popularity signals and interpretation limits.

- [Audience and persona map](audiences.md): psychographic dimensions, gamer circumstances, creator/contributor users and possible payer roles.
- [Jobs to be done](jobs-to-be-done.md): hypothetical player, creator/operator and paid-service progress, adoption forces and observable outcomes.

The four initial maps have undergone evidence-boundary and peer review. No hypothesis has been selected or validated as a market conclusion.
