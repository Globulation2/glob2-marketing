# Analysis: marketing possibilities

[Repository overview](../README.md) · [Task ledger](../planning/tasks.md) · [Facts](../facts/README.md)

This section explores hypotheses and alternatives, separate from the factual estate inventory. It does not represent validated market demand or decisions.

Planned coverage: gamer psychographics; gamer and user personas; jobs to be done; marketing channels; existing and potential business models; historical and current comparable games; and the pros, cons, dependencies, and uncertainties of different approaches. Research documents will be linked here as they are completed.

Initial work must report possibilities without recommendations or firm conclusions.
