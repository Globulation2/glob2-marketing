# Paid placement: Push Your App (Gamezebo network)

[Analysis index](README.md) · [Mobile press outreach ledger](../planning/mobile-press-outreach.md#gamezebo-reply-and-paid-placement-offer--2026-11-25-to-2026-11-26) · [Mobile games press](mobile-games-press.md)

Researched 2026-11-26 after the Gamezebo review pitch was forwarded to the Push Your App sales team. **User decision (2026-11-26): not worth buying.** The user's words: "nah it doesn't sound like its worth it". The user also said a global audience matters more than a US one.

Public web research was done by a research sub-agent on 2026-11-26. Traffic figures are third-party estimates. Prices marked "reseller" were not quoted by Push Your App.

## What Push Your App is

- **Verified:** Push Your App is a UK mobile-app promotion agency. It sells editorial pieces, display ads, site takeovers, consultancy (including mock reviews) and press-release mail-outs. Its site publishes no prices. [pushyourapp.com](https://pushyourapp.com)
- **Verified:** Gamezebo's and Droid Gamers' advertise pages both say Push Your App exclusively manages their advertising and advertorial sales. It sells for these sites but does not own them. Gamezebo has been owned by iWin since 2016. [Gamezebo advertise](https://www.gamezebo.com/advertise/) · [Droid Gamers advertise](https://www.droidgamers.com/advertise/) · [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gamezebo)
- **Verified:** Its current partner list includes Gamezebo, Droid Gamers, Android Headlines, AppAdvice, TouchTapPlay, Download Free Games, iFanzine and some French, German, Spanish and Russian sites. The list does **not** include GamingonPhone or App Gamer, although the sales email named both.
- **Verified:** The same sales pitch went to the open-source FreeDroid project's mailing list in January 2023. The offer is routine outreach, not specific interest in Globulation 2. [FreeDroid list](https://sourceforge.net/p/freedroid/mailman/freedroid-discussion/thread/CAFULXAwRvipuFJTyxGe=tDrn9EV7RPfa6BpdrXNS+GA2VMSfNg@mail.gmail.com/)
- **Verified:** Sending a free review pitch on to an ad seller fits the standard advertorial model.
- **Not found:** no scam reports. Also no first-hand client accounts, postmortems or install results. The site shows client logos (Ubisoft, Tencent, Zimad and others) but no case studies.

## Sponsored-content labelling

- **Verified:** a July 2017 Gamezebo piece about GamerCal carries a footer saying it is sponsored and supplied by the Push Your App network. [Example](https://www.gamezebo.com/sponsored/interview-how-gamercal-offers-a-unique-way-to-keep-your-mobile-gaming-super-streamlined/)
- **Verified:** Gamezebo's Sponsored category stops at December 2017. [Category](https://www.gamezebo.com/category/sponsored/)
- **Unverified (article undated, page returned 403):** TechRaptor reported paid pieces on Droid Gamers, AppAdvice and TouchTapPlay that were not marked sponsored. [TechRaptor](https://techraptor.net/content/android-gaming-site-droid-gamers-offers-paid-reviews-advertorials)
- **Inference:** paid posts since 2017 are either rare or filed without that label.

## Assessment

These are inferences, not measured results.

- **The audience is real but small and poorly matched.** Most visitors arrive from search looking for guides to other games. Few are strategy players browsing for something new. Invalid or fake traffic is not the main risk.
- **Value is low at the quoted price.** At $250 for one article, the cost is likely tens of dollars per thousand real visitors, with few installs expected.
- **Any future test needs measurement.** Use App Store Connect campaign links and Play Console UTM links, keep spend at about $150 or less, and require a clear "Sponsored" label.
- **Free alternatives are better value.** Pitch the network's editorial writers directly; the sales reply supplied their contacts and said most coverage is unpaid. Others: strategy-focused outlets such as Pocket Tactics, open-source and strategy communities, and measurable small Apple Search Ads or Google App campaign tests.
