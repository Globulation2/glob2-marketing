# Storefront v2 translation review record

[Planning index](README.md) · [Storefront v2 rollout](storefront-v2-rollout.md) · [Canonical translations](../translations/README.md) · [Field map](../translations/storefront-v2-field-map.md) · [Glossary](../translations/glossary.md)

## Method and limits

- **What was done.** On 2026-11-24 the storefront-v2 English listing ([en.md](../translations/en.md), 78 fields) was translated into all 44 non-English locales by a workflow of 88 agent runs: 44 dedicated translator agents, each followed by an independent reviewer agent that compared the catalog with the English, edited it and ran `storefront.py review-record`. Identities are `storefront-v2-translator-<locale>` and `storefront-v2-reviewer-<locale>`.
- **What it is not.** This is AI translation with independent AI linguistic review. No human and no native speaker has read any of these catalogs. A rating below is the reviewing agent's own judgement of its locale, not an outside assessment.
- **Where the review status lives.** The hash-bound review records in [`translations/reviews/`](../translations/reviews/) bind each review to the exact English source and translation hashes. Editing a field stales its record; `python3 translations/tools/storefront.py check --require-review` is the gate. This document is the narrative record and does not replace those files.
- **How this file was made.** It is generated by script from the reviewers' structured results (one object per locale: rating, fields changed, serious errors found, residual concerns). Reviewer text is verbatim, including process remarks and the reviewers' own reminders that no native speaker was involved. Three mechanical changes only: `*` is escaped for Markdown; the absolute local repository path is shortened to a repository-relative one; and entries that only state that no errors were found are not counted as errors or concerns and are listed separately, in full, as the reviewer's overall statement.
- **How to read the counts.** "Serious errors fixed" counts the entries a reviewer returned under that heading, less the no-errors-found statements. It is not a count of confirmed meaning errors: several reviewers say outright that their listed items are moderate or quality fixes (for example `eo`, `nb`, `bg`, `sr-Cyrl`, `tr`, `pt-BR`), and one entry can cover several fields. "Residual concerns" likewise includes process notes.
- **A note on the reviewers' briefs.** Several reviewers remark that their catalog already held a storefront-v2 draft rather than the v1 text their brief described. That is the expected order of the workflow (the translator ran first); the remarks are kept as written.

## Patterns worth a human check

Derived by reading all 44 results. Locale lists name the reviewers who raised the point; a locale missing from a list was not flagged, which is not the same as checked and clear.

- **"No in-app purchases" on the 28-character overlay and 32-character trailer line.** The store's own term for in-app purchases does not fit beside "no ads" in most languages, so `shot_overlay_fair_deal` and `trailer_line_4` carry a shorter form than the body text. Bare "purchases", which is wider than the English and which the glossary warns against: `hu`, `eu`, `es`, `el`, `ro`, `sw`, `ta` (overlay and trailer line), `sl`, `pt-PT` (overlay only). "In-game purchases" instead of the store term: `cs`, `pl`, `sk`, `ru`, `sr-Cyrl`, `tr`, `ca` (`uk` uses the in-game form in every field). "Microtransactions": `fi`, `bg`. A shortened or alternative form of the store term: `ar`, `eo`, `fa`, `ms`, `bn`, `te`, `ur`, `id`. Other compromises to fit: a different word for ads on the overlay than everywhere else (`nl`, `it`, `sr-Cyrl`), a slash or ampersand (`id`, `ms`), singular nouns (`pt-BR`). The Italian draft had the bare-"purchases" widening and the reviewer restored "in-app"; the Malay draft said "app purchases" and was corrected.
- **One negation meant to cover two nouns.** The Persian overlay and trailer line could be read as "ad-free, and in-app purchases", the opposite of the claim; the reviewer rewrote both. The Vietnamese reviewer reports the same possible misreading still present in `shot_overlay_fair_deal` (no unambiguous wording fits 28 characters). The Swedish and German reviewers note a single "without" governing both nouns and judge that the normal, unambiguous reading.
- **"67 map generators" shortened on `shot_overlay_maps`.** Bare "generators" with "map" left implied: `de`, `pt-BR`, `pt-PT`, `fr`, `es`, `it`, `nl`, `ru`, `uk`, `fi`, `bg`, `te`. The Dutch, Finnish and Telugu reviewers note the bare word can also mean power or electrical generators. Replaced by "random landscapes" or "random kinds", so that generation is only implied: `el`, `sl`, `sw`, `ta`, `sv`. Label-style or coined wording: `ro`, `fa`, `th`, `bn` (mixes two words for "map").
- **Renderings of "premium currency".** Raised by the reviewer in 35 of 44 locales. Twenty use the gamer loan term or a calque of "premium currency" (`de`, `cs`, `da`, `pt-BR`, `hu`, `ca`, `fr`, `sk`, `sv`, `nl`, `el`, `ro`, `sr-Cyrl`, `nb`, `ms`, `ur`, `bg`, `hi`, `uk`, `fil`); fifteen use a descriptive "paid" or "purchasable currency" (`eo`, `eu`, `ar`, `fa`, `ru`, `sl`, `ko`, `zh-Hans`, `zh-Hant`, `ja`, `vi`, `sw`, `te`, `th`, `ta`). The shared question is whether a casual store visitor reads it as currency bought with real money, and not as "the game has no resources"; the glossary's fallback "no currency to buy" is available where the term proves unclear. The Persian draft's first rendering was open to a foreign-exchange reading and was changed. "Loot boxes" raises the same loanword-or-local-term question in `da`, `eo`, `eu`, `fr`, `ro`, `sl`, `sr-Cyrl`, `ko`, `ja`, `zh-Hans`, `zh-Hant`, `tr`, `fi`, `ms`.
- **"Computer opponent" kept apart from "AI agent".** Every reviewer who addresses it reports the two kept distinct. Points to check: (a) the short overlay form on `shot_overlay_opponents` can be parsed as "opponents of the computer" or "opponents on a computer" in `pt-BR`, `pt-PT`, `ca` and `eu`, is telegraphic in `fr`, less idiomatic in `ro`, and differs from the body term in `it` (fixed from the misparse); `es` uses "CPU", and the Japanese and Thai reviewers note players say "CPU" or "bot"; (b) "desktop players" in `multiplayer_crossplay` read as computer-controlled players and was fixed in `ca`, `pt-PT`, `sw`; (c) "play with a computer opponent" was ambiguous between with and against and was fixed in `tr`, `ur`, `hi`, `bn`; (d) the game's own interface calls its computer players "AI" in `pt-BR`, `el`, `zh-Hant`, `tr`, `sr-Cyrl` and `sl`, so the listing deliberately differs from the in-game menu there.
- **The word for "AI agent" and the co-play overlay.** The abbreviation is unsettled between a local form and Latin "AI" in `eu`, `hu`, `it`, `sl`, `nb`, `bg`; the noun is unsettled in `el`, `zh-Hant`, `vi`, `sw`. `sl`, `nb` and `th` use the Latin or English form in the hidden `windows_search_term_7` while the visible copy uses the local form. On `shot_overlay_ai_coplay` and `trailer_line_ai_coplay`, "your" is dropped in `pl`, `uk`, `hi`, `vi`; the line has no verb in `eu`, `el`; it is a statement instead of an imperative in `tr`, `fil`. The calque "play the same colony" was replaced in `sl`, `pt-PT`, `nb`, `ur`, `hi`, `bn`, `te`, `mr`.
- **"Colony" dropped from `ios_subtitle` and `shot_overlay_hero`.** The full genre term leaves no room in 30 and 28 characters, so the subtitle or hero overlay is plain "real-time strategy" without "colony" in `ar`, `bg`, `ca`, `cs`, `es`, `eu`, `fi`, `fil`, `fr`, `it`, `pt-BR`, `pt-PT`, `ro`, `ru`, `sk`, `sl`, `sr-Cyrl`, `tr`; `ta` drops it on the overlay only; `bn` keeps "colony" in the subtitle by dropping "real-time" and does the reverse on the overlay. "Colony" is not in `ios_keywords` either, because the field map's concept list excludes it (noted by `cs`, `fr`, `pt-PT`). Locales that kept it did so with a coined compound or an adapted phrase a marketer may want to revisit: `de`, `hu`, `nb`, `nl`, `da`, `sv`, `fa`, `pl`, `ur`, `th`.
- **Other cuts forced by short fields.** `play_short_description` drops "offline" (the field map's first cut) in `eu`, `ca`, `fr`, `es`, `it`, `ru`, `pt-PT`, `uk`, `fi`, `vi`; `pt-BR`, `el`, `sr-Cyrl`, `bg`, `bn` instead keep "offline" and shorten the colony-not-every-unit contrast. `short_pitch` and `desktop_short_pitch` omit the sentence "You set the priorities; your colony does the work." in `el` and `fi`. `appstream_summary` drops "rivals" in `ca`, `es`, `fi`. `trailer_line_3` is an adaptation or a noun phrase, not the English imperative, in `ro`, `sr-Cyrl`, `fi`, `bn`.
- **In-app purchase term differs between stores.** One shared field set serves every store, so where Apple and Google use different local labels the Apple-facing text (including `short_pitch` as App Store promotional text) does not carry Apple's exact term, or the reverse: `pt-BR`, `ca`, `es`, `sv`, `zh-Hant`, `zh-Hans`, `ru`, `ko`, `ja`, `pt-PT`. The store label could not be verified in `uk`, `ur`, `fil`, `fi` and `th`. `bn` was changed to Google Play's live term.
- **Right-to-left embedding and script rendering.** The Urdu reviewer rewrote eight fields so they open with an Urdu word, because a field that begins with a Latin-script term can flip paragraph direction where a store auto-detects it; `epic_legal`, `trailer_title` and `itch_downloads_note` still begin with a Latin name in `ur`, and `epic_legal` does in `fa` and `ar`. The Arabic reviewer asks for a platform preview of every field with embedded Latin terms (MCP, JavaScript, iPhone/iPad, URLs). For the caption and trailer renderers: Thai has no word spaces and the catalog contains no zero-width spaces, so line breaking must be Thai-aware; `bn` now uses Bengali digits and `fa` Persian digits, so fonts must cover them; Devanagari conjuncts in `hi` can render wider than the character count suggests. `fr` uses a no-break space (U+00A0) before colons and semicolons, which a console may normalise and show as a whitespace-only difference on readback.
- **Apple silicon wording in `mac_tail`.** Changed to follow Apple's local spelling in `hu` and `sk`, and reworded to store phrasing in `nb`. Not verified against Apple's current local pages in `cs`, `da`, `ar`, `el`, `uk`, `th`; capitalisation ("Apple silicon" or "Apple Silicon") and the chip-or-processor noun vary between catalogs. `es` avoids the regional un Mac / una Mac split by omitting the article.
- **Keyword choices are not backed by search-volume data.** No reviewer had App Store or Microsoft Store search data; reviewers in about 40 of the 44 locales say explicitly that `ios_keywords` and the `windows_search_term_*` fields are judgement. Recurring moves: adding the local "without internet" (or "wifi") phrase as the search form of "offline", which some reviewers note is not literally on the field map's concept list (`cs`, `sr-Cyrl`, `nl`, `zh-Hans`, `zh-Hant`, `te`); dropping LAN, map editor, economy, tactics or skirmish for space; hidden search terms deliberately spelled as people type rather than as the visible copy spells them (`sl`, `nb`, `tr`, `hi`, `fa`, `fi`, `th`). Reviewers for `eo`, `eu`, `sr-Cyrl`, `ur`, `mr`, `hi`, `ta`, `bn`, `sw`, `fil` and `th` note that players may search in English, in Latin script or in another language, or that the store has no localisation for the language. How Apple tokenises or folds the script is unverified for `ar`, `vi`, `th` and `el`; compound subtitles in `nl`, `hu` and `nb` will not index their component words.
- **The game's own terms against plainer words.** The glossary requires in-game names, and several read oddly in store copy: building names in `de`, `cs`, `ru`, `tr`, `ca`, `sl`, `ms`, `bg`, `mr`, `pl`, `bn`; the word for the 67 landscapes in `tr`, `ur`, `te`, `fil`, `mr`. The creature name "globs" differs from the in-game name in `cs`, `pl`, `nl`, `ko`, and has an awkward homonym or near-homonym in `sr-Cyrl`, `sl`, `pl`, `fa`, `ta`; `ur` was switched to the in-game name. The word for "colony" differs from the in-game word in `fa`, `hi`, `sw`, and the in-game word carries a colonial-history or other everyday connotation in `ko`, `vi`, `bn`, `ta`. "Casual" follows the game interface but can read as "with friends" in `hu`, `eu`, `pl`, `fr` (fixed for that reason in `cs`), and players often use the English loanwords for ranked and casual (`da`, `nl`, `sv`, `sr-Cyrl`, `el`, `ms`, `ur`). "Quick match" contains a speed word the glossary cautions about in `de`, `da`, `nl`, `ko`, `ur`, `mr`.
- **`epic_tail` and `epic_legal` against the text saved in the Epic console.** The field map marks both as unchanged from storefront-v1, and most reviewers left them byte-identical. Three catalogs now differ from v1: `zh-Hans` (a carried-over v1 meaning error, "the player must create an account", corrected, and the guest term aligned), `sv` (the translator's grammatical corrections kept) and `bn` (one term in `epic_legal`). If the v1 text is saved in the Epic console for those locales, it differs from the catalog. Small inconsistencies between the retained v1 legal text and the new v2 fields were noted and left in `ro`, `ru`, `sr-Cyrl`, `id`, `zh-Hant`, `mr`, `tr`, `ko`, `vi`, `ur`.
- **Register and regional choices.** Formal address is used throughout in `cs`, `el`, `bg`, `tr`, `sl`; the `cs`, `el`, `bg` and `tr` reviewers note that some mobile listings use the informal form; `id` uses informal address where the game uses formal. `es` pairs a Latin American and a European Spanish word in `ai_body` while being uploaded to es-ES slots.
- **Fields sitting exactly at their limit.** Many overlays (28), trailer lines (32), captions (100), `play_short_description` (80) and `ios_keywords` (100 bytes) are at or within a character or two of the limit; reviewers for `ar`, `eo`, `eu`, `fa`, `fil`, `fr`, `hu`, `id`, `mr`, `ms`, `nl`, `pl`, `ta`, `te`, `th`, `vi` ask that any later edit be recounted, and `nb` and `hi` ask that the rendered images be checked for clipping.
- **Error classes the reviewers did fix, as a guide to what may remain elsewhere.** "Play 23 maps" reading as playing cards (`nl`, `bg`) or as maps being the thing played (`ur`, `sw`); "67 random landscapes" reading as "67 random countries" (`sk`) or "67 uneven terrains" (`ta`); "where rival colonies meet" reading as "where they are located" (`ca`, `pt-PT`, `ur`); a modifier attached to the wrong noun in `feature_scripting` (`es`, `pt-BR`, `eu`); added promises or intensifiers (`ja`, `sl`, `hu`, `pl`, `ko`); idioms and homographs with an unintended meaning (`sl`, `fa`, `tr`, `ur`, `ko`).

## Totals

- **Ratings.** 7 of 44 locales rated publishable (`de`, `es`, `fr`, `id`, `ja`, `sv`, `zh-Hant`); 37 of 44 rated publishable with concerns. No locale was rated lower.
- **Serious errors fixed.** 171 entries in 30 locales; 14 locales reported none (`ar`, `da`, `de`, `el`, `fi`, `fr`, `id`, `mr`, `ro`, `sv`, `te`, `th`, `uk`, `vi`). In 21 of those 30 locales the reviewer added a statement that no error, or no further error, of the classes named in the brief was found; see "How to read the counts" above.
- **Fields changed by reviewers.** 569 field edits across all 44 locales (of 78 fields each); every reviewer changed at least 3 and at most 33.
- **Residual concerns.** 517 entries across all 44 locales.

## Per-locale results

### ar — Arabic

Rating: **Publishable with concerns**. Catalog: [ar.md](../translations/ar.md). Review record: [reviews/ar.json](../translations/reviews/ar.json).

Fields the reviewer changed (9): `headline`, `short_pitch`, `ios_keywords`, `control_body`, `defense_body`, `multiplayer_online_body`, `android_tail`, `ios_tail`, `shot_caption_fair_deal`.

**Serious errors found and fixed (0)**

- None reported.

**Residual concerns for a native speaker (12)**

- shot_overlay_ai_coplay / trailer_line_ai_coplay: 'العب مع وكيل ذكائك الاصطناعي' is grammatical (possessive on the second term of the compound, like 'جواز سفرك') and exactly 28 characters, but a native speaker should confirm it reads naturally. 'العب بجانب وكيل ذكاء اصطناعي' also fits 28 but loses 'your'.
- shot_overlay_fair_deal / trailer_line_4 use the short form 'مشتريات داخلية' because the store term 'عمليات شراء داخل التطبيق' cannot fit in 28 characters with 'no ads'. A native speaker should confirm players read it as in-app purchases. The body fields use the full store term.
- 'premium currency' is rendered 'عملات مدفوعة' (paid currencies) in fair_deal_line and shot_caption_fair_deal. I believe this is the usual gamer wording, but the glossary fallback 'بدون عملات تُشترى' (no currency to buy) is available if a native reader finds it unclear.
- 'globs' is 'الكريات', the name the game's own Arabic catalog (texts.ar.txt) uses. It is a translation ('globules') rather than a transliteration; I kept it for consistency with the in-game text.
- 'النُزل' is used as the plural of inn, following the game's Arabic catalog; the dictionary plural is 'أنزال'. A native speaker may want to confirm.
- ios_subtitle and shot_overlay_hero are 'استراتيجية في الوقت الحقيقي' (27 characters): 'colony' is dropped because it cannot fit with the full genre term.
- ios_keywords (94 of 100 bytes) is now split into single words so the App Store can combine them ('بدون' + 'إعلانات' / 'نت'). It uses standard hamza spelling ('إعلانات'); many users type 'اعلانات', and I could not verify whether App Store search normalises alef forms. Economy, tactics and map editor are left out for space.
- mac_tail keeps 'Apple silicon' in Latin letters. Apple's Arabic pages may write 'سيليكون Apple'; I could not verify this, so check against Apple's current Arabic wording.
- shot_caption_fair_deal is exactly at the 100-character limit and the two overlay fields above are exactly at 28; any later edit must recount.
- 'لعبة محلية' / 'الألعاب المحلية' (local game) follows the game's own Arabic string, but out of context it could be read as 'locally made game' in shot_caption_ai_coplay.
- epic_legal (unchanged v1 text) starts with the Latin product name, so in auto-direction contexts the paragraph may render left-to-right; RTL rendering of all fields with embedded Latin terms (MCP, JavaScript, iPhone/iPad, URLs) still needs a platform preview check.
- multiplayer_heading uses the plural imperative 'العبوا معًا' while the rest of the listing addresses a single player; this is natural for 'play together' but is a deliberate register exception.

**Reviewer's overall statement**

- No serious meaning, policy or limit errors were found in the translator's draft; my nine edits are clarity and naturalness fixes. All 78 fields were back-translated against en.md, but this is AI review, not native-speaker validation.

### bg — Bulgarian

Rating: **Publishable with concerns**. Catalog: [bg.md](../translations/bg.md). Review record: [reviews/bg.json](../translations/reviews/bg.json).

Fields the reviewer changed (14): `short_pitch`, `desktop_short_pitch`, `play_short_description`, `ios_keywords`, `map_body`, `multiplayer_online_body`, `ai_body`, `open_source_body`, `android_tail`, `ios_tail`, `mac_tail`, `itch_downloads_note`, `shot_overlay_territory`, `shot_caption_build`.

**Serious errors found and fixed (8)**

- map_body: 'Играйте на 23 ръчно изработени карти' echoes the idiom 'играя на карти' (play cards); reworded to 'Играйте на някоя от 23-те ръчно изработени карти' so it reads only as maps
- shot_overlay_territory: 'Изберете накъде да растете' read as 'choose which way you grow' and did not match the expansion wording elsewhere; now 'Решете къде да се разширите' (27 characters)
- android_tail / ios_tail / mac_tail: 'Играта за един играч работи офлайн' could read as 'the game, which is for one player'; now 'Режимът за един играч работи офлайн'
- open_source_body: 'Globulation 2 започна през 2001 г.' was unidiomatic and blurred the two facts; now 'Работата по Globulation 2 започна през 2001 г., а през 2004 г. играта стана публично достъпна'
- itch_downloads_note: 'В itch.io MCP сървърът' ran two Latin names together so 'itch.io MCP' looked like one name; sentence restructured
- play_short_description: bare 'не единици' was clipped; now 'не единиците' (exactly 80 characters, keeps genre, no ads, the contrast and offline)
- ios_keywords: replaced 'тактика' and 'LAN' with 'интернет' so that the common Bulgarian offline query 'без интернет' is matched together with the existing 'без' (97 of 100 bytes)
- ai_body: dropped a redundant pronoun ('какво прави той'); multiplayer_online_body: 'влезте с акаунт' changed to 'влезте в акаунта си' and future tense for the computer-opponent fallback; short_pitch / desktop_short_pitch: added the conjunction 'а' for a natural contrast; shot_caption_build: imperative to match the English and the other captions

**Residual concerns for a native speaker (11)**

- shot_overlay_fair_deal and trailer_line_4 use 'микроплащания' (microtransactions) for in-app purchases because the store term 'покупки в приложението' cannot fit in 28/32 characters; a native speaker should confirm this is the best short equivalent ('микротранзакции' is one character too long for the overlay).
- 'премиум валута' (fair_deal_line, shot_caption_fair_deal) is the gamer loan term for premium currency; a native speaker should confirm it is clear to casual players and does not need 'валута за купуване'.
- 'ИИ агент' is used throughout for AI agent (matching the game's own 'ИИ'); Bulgarian tech press also writes 'AI агент'. A native speaker may prefer the Latin form, especially in windows_search_term_7.
- play_short_description is exactly 80 characters and shortens the contrast to 'не единиците' in order to keep 'Офлайн'; the field map's strict cut order would drop 'offline' first. Alternative at 79 characters: 'Стратегия в реално време без реклами. Командвате колонията, а не всяка единица.'
- ios_keywords: 'интернет' is included only to form the offline query 'без интернет'; LAN, tactics, multiplayer, economy and map editor are left out for space. Real App Store search volumes for Bulgarian were not checked.
- ios_subtitle and shot_overlay_hero are 'Стратегия в реално време' with no 'colony', because a version with 'колония' does not fit in 30/28 characters.
- shot_overlay_maps ' 23 карти и 67 генератора' relies on the picture for 'generators' to be read as map generators; 'генератора на карти' does not fit in 28.
