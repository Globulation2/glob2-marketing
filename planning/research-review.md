## Findings for the initial handoff

- The inspected estate contains five public organization repositories, modern website/application surfaces, historical wiki/forum/IRC/Savannah records, and independent catalogs. Discovery does not establish publishing access or active communities.
- The asset ledger records 21 families. One game/website screenshot pair is byte-identical; Android portrait, website landscape/replay and historical sets are separate. Existing copy foregrounds different aspects of the game. No full submission-console image comparison was performed.
- The observed website offers 0.11.4.0 as experimental, Snap displays stable 0.11.2.0, and several legacy/distro surfaces retain older lineages. Each is a dated observation of that surface.
- Source evidence includes sixteen gameplay/creator capability groups and five commercial machinery groups. Optional skins, AI credits and reporting implementations are not verified live sales, demand or enabled services.
- The initial chronology distinguishes the 1999–2000 predecessor, Glob2 development beginning in 2001, first-alpha records in 2004, beta/hosting changes and modern releases. Historical gaps remain explicit.
- Analysis separates gamer, creator/user and payer circumstances. It records nine psychographic dimensions, eight gamer and six user roles, fourteen player/creator/operator jobs, seventeen channel families, seventeen business-model possibilities and twelve comparison cases. These are hypotheses and reference cases, not measured segment sizes or selected strategies.
