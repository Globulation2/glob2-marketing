## Who to find

Three adults who have recently played a strategy, colony-management or logistics game and have never played Glob2. Prefer at least one direct-command RTS player and one colony/logistics player; do not turn that balance into a difficult quota. Avoid filling all three places with friends, contributors or people already enthusiastic about Glob2. A referral can qualify, but record that relationship and recruitment source. Creator-specific messaging is outside this small player round.

## Invitation — copy after permission

**Title:** Glob2 / Globulation 2: looking for 3 strategy players for a 15-minute description test

> I'm working on Glob2 / Globulation 2 and would like to talk with three adults who have recently played a strategy, colony-management or logistics game, but have never played Glob2.
>
> You'll read a few short descriptions and tell me what you think the game would be like and what interests or confuses you. About 15 minutes over a voice call; no installation, purchase or recording. This is a voluntary, unpaid conversation. Critical feedback is welcome, and you can skip questions or stop at any time. With your permission, we'll keep anonymous notes; contact details won't go in our public research repository.
>
> Interested? Reply or message me with a recent strategy game you've played and whether you've played Glob2 before. Please don't post your email or availability publicly. I'll arrange a time privately with selected volunteers. Please wait until the conversation to look up the game, so we can hear your first impressions.

No game link or positioning variant is included before the session, to reduce priming. Use this invitation only where permitted; do not omit a community's required disclosures or links to preserve the study design. If a community requires gameplay promotion, use another source for this round.

## Minimal private screener and selection

1. Are you 18 or older? (Yes/no; no birth date.)
2. What strategy, colony-management or logistics game have you played recently? What did you spend most of your time doing in it?
3. Have you ever played Glob2 / Globulation 2? If not, have you seen or read much about it?
4. Can you join a 15-minute voice call and view short text cards? What time zone and two possible times work for you?

Ask availability only after basic eligibility, using the volunteer's existing reply channel. Select three eligible people with differing recent game experience; do not select based on agreement with a message. Record only P1–P3, recruitment source, relevant game experience and prior familiarity in research notes. Keep handles, contact details and scheduling outside this public repository. Do not promise compensation, a giveaway or future access.

If volunteers do not respond, report the gap before adding channels, incentives or a larger process. Three conversations are the scope; stop recruiting when three are arranged.
