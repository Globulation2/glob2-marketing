## Long-description draft

### Build a thriving colony in contested territory

Food, workers and space shape every expansion in Globulation 2. Build a sprawling colony, balance its competing needs, and contend with rivals for land and resources in a real-time strategy game about making the whole colony work.

### Grow an economy that can sustain your ambitions

A growing colony needs more than buildings. Food, construction, training and other work compete for the same workforce. Decide where workers are needed, build the services your colony depends on, and keep growth in balance with what you can sustain.

### Make the map part of your plan

Where you build matters. Shape your base around the land and resources, choose where to expand, and consider what each new foothold means for your economy and your rivals. A thriving colony needs both the means to grow and room to do it.

### Guide the colony as a whole

Set worker demand at buildings and place flags to direct activity. Your creatures find their own routes and attend to their needs, letting you manage an expanding colony through its priorities. Watch how your decisions affect the whole system, then adapt as its needs change.

### Build under pressure

Rival colonies have ambitions of their own. Expansion brings competition for land and resources, and your economy must support both growth and defense. Develop your colony while preparing for the conflicts that can decide the match.

Globulation 2 is free and open source.

## Feature bullets

- **An interdependent colony economy:** balance food, workers, construction and training as your population grows.
- **Spatial strategy:** make base placement, resources and expansion part of your plan.
- **Autonomous creatures:** guide activity through building worker demand and flags while units handle their own movement and needs.
- **Rival pressure:** sustain your colony while competing for land and resources and preparing for warfare.
- **Free and open source:** explore a strategy game whose source is available to inspect and contribute to.
