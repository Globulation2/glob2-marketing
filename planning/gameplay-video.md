# Gameplay video for marketing

[Planning index](README.md) · [Launch positioning](../analysis/positioning-v1.md) · [Reddit media plan](reddit-campaign/media.md)

For trailers, store media, release posts, social clips, and other promotional placements, lead with a moment that shows why a match is exciting to watch and play. Use footage of the actual game. A busy mid-game match between computer opponents works well because the camera can show several parts of the colony and a fight at the same time.

## Choose a scene

- Look for developed colonies with many visible buildings and units. Workers should be moving between jobs while warriors are advancing, attacking, or defending nearby.
- Start with visible activity. Put troop movement or an attack in the opening seconds and include enough of the exchange to show what happens.
- Pull the camera back enough to show the buildup and the contested area together. Check that individual units, buildings, and attacks are still recognizable at the size people will see in a feed.
- Use the actual match, interface, and gameplay speed. Label AI versus AI footage as a spectator view and record the build, platform, map, and capture conditions. Describe only the action the selected segment shows.

## Review the clip

Watch the opening, middle, and end at normal speed and preview the encoded export at feed size. Check that a viewer unfamiliar with Globulation 2 can see both colonies at work and identify where the fighting is happening. If the scene needs an explanation to find the action, pick a stronger segment or adjust the camera. Use tutorials and single-mechanic clips for player guides and follow-up explanations.
