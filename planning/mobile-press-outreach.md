## Review before outreach

1. Confirm the launch event from the [publishing ledger](approved-storefront-rollout.md#publishing-and-review-submission-authorized--2026-10-30) and the local storefront. Record the verified platform, regional availability, version, date and public store URL. Use the actual stage, such as closed test, preorder or public release. Check language support, device compatibility, pricing and controls when mentioned. Hold drafts that depend on any unresolved local fact.
2. Replace every bracketed field with a verified detail or remove the optional sentence. Test store, press pack and gameplay links from a recipient's perspective. Use media from the [approved asset manifest](../assets/storefront-approved-v1/README.md) and identify the build shown when a video or screenshot makes a platform-specific claim.
3. Confirm review access before offering it. Keep redemption codes, access tokens, credentials, private addresses and review arrangements out of this public repository.
4. Confirm the recipient and intake route on its published contact page. Use one route per publication and identify the intended regional edition if the inbox is shared. Coordinate Pocket Gamer, its French edition and 148Apps because they share inboxes or editorial staff. For general forms and shared partnership contacts, check whether editorial submissions are accepted before using a release pitch.
5. Review localized copy in context and verify that the subject also matches the release stage. Keep recipient status and any send receipts with authorized outreach records. The drafts are ready for internal review, with external use contingent on confirmed release facts and authorization.
