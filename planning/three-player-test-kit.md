## Message cards — v1

### Card A

**Set the priorities. The colony gets to work.**

Globulation 2 is a free, open-source real-time strategy game where you set worker demand at buildings and place flags. Your globs find their own routes and meet their needs while you balance the colony's work, expansion and rivals.

### Card B

**Food, workers, expansion. Make the colony work.**

Build and sustain a colony of autonomous globs. Set work priorities, keep food and training in balance, and expand while rival colonies pursue their own goals. Globulation 2 turns the colony economy into a real-time strategy contest.

### Card C

**A free, open-source colony RTS.**

Globulation 2 is a free, open-source real-time strategy game. Direct a colony of autonomous globs through work priorities, food, training and expansion while rival colonies pursue their own goals.

C is a source-derived shortened variant of [candidate C](../analysis/positioning-options.md#c--strategy-you-can-keep), using the [documented gameplay](../facts/product.md#gameplay-and-player-surfaces). A also mentions free/open-source access, so this tests different emphases in complete descriptions, not the isolated effect of licensing. Differences in wording and length are part of the drafts, not controlled experimental variables.

## Fifteen-minute script

Assign the order before sessions: **P01 A → B → C; P02 B → C → A; P03 C → A → B.** Each card gets one first exposure. That is too little evidence for numerical comparison; later responses are informed by earlier cards.

| Time | Facilitator wording and action |
| --- | --- |
| 0–1 min | “We're checking descriptions of a strategy game. We're testing the wording, and criticism is useful. This takes about 15 minutes; you can skip anything or stop. Is it okay if I take anonymous notes and summarize them without identifying you in our public project notes? I won't record audio or video.” If they decline, do not retain or publish their responses. Do not promise compensation unless separately arranged and disclosed. |
| 1–2 min | “What's the most recent strategy or colony game you played? What kept you interested, or made you stop?” Confirm no prior Glob2 play; note any familiarity with its descriptions. |
| 2–5 min | Show the assigned first card silently. “In your own words, what do you think you would do in this game?” Then: “What sounds interesting or uninteresting to you? What words gave you that impression?” Before explaining anything: “What would you expect a session to be like—what creates pressure, and what carries over afterward?” Record the expectation even if wrong. |
| 5–7 min | Hide the first card and show the second. “What does this description make you expect? What sounds interesting or uninteresting, and why?” Do not explain or correct yet. |
| 7–9 min | Hide the second card and show the third. Ask the same two questions. |
| 9–11 min | Show all three. “Which, if any, would make you want to find out more? What would you look for next? Is there anything here that would put you off?” Accept “none”; avoid asking them to pick a winner to be helpful. |
| 11–13 min | Read the constant factual clarification below. “What, if anything, differs from what you expected? Does that change your interest? Why?” Keep these answers separate from initial reactions. |
| 13–15 min | “What would you need to see before deciding whether to try it?” Clarify any remaining material misunderstanding using documented facts; acknowledge unknowns. Ask: “May we include a short anonymous quotation from your answers in our public project notes?” Record their choice; thank them. No signup or installation task. |

**Constant factual clarification, read only at minute 11:**

> Players set worker demand at buildings and place flags. The globs move and meet needs autonomously. Food, training and other work compete for workers. Rival colonies and match objectives create pressure. This describes a real-time strategy match, rather than promising an endless peaceful settlement. The core game is free and open source; that does not promise every optional hosted service is free. We haven't tested an installation or play route in this conversation.

This clarification follows [P01–P03 and commercial-scope evidence](../facts/product.md). It is a verbal explanation, not gameplay proof. If asked about match length, supported devices, saves or live services, do not guess; record the question for follow-up. Do not teach the intended interpretation until after the comparison.
