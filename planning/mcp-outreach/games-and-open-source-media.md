# Games and open-source media research

[Outreach project](README.md) · [Existing mobile press ledger](../mobile-press-outreach.md) · [Product facts](../../facts/product.md)

Research checked **2026-11-23**. The shortlist has **8 prioritized outlets** in two waves, **5 follow-up outlets**, and **6 holds**. Bylines identify people with relevant work. Use the published route for the outlet unless the source provides an appropriate direct route. No outlet was contacted as part of this research. Priorities reflect editorial fit and the proof each outlet would need. Interest and coverage are unverified.

For the near-term angle, show a player directing a colony with an optional desktop MCP client, and separately show someone editing or AI-assisting a JavaScript colony bot and testing the resulting behavior locally. The game already has ordinary internet multiplayer. The new separated MCP matchmaking queues and ladders are a stronger later story once a compatible public build and server are verified with two human players. Keep the scope visible in any video or copy. ## What this audience needs to see

- **Games desks** need one legible RTS tradeoff and its outcome on video. Capture the input beside the game, then clearly identify the player’s and the agent’s decisions.
- **Specialist strategy and podcast leads** can use a repeatable scenario, an open question and a replay. A close failure can make a better discussion than a promotional win.
- **Open-source leads** need a released Linux artifact, source and reproducible instructions. Describe the boundary between desktop MCP, the external client/model and the versioned in-game script, including which actions run locally.
- **Online coverage** needs two separately connected participants, the public version and mode, an explanation of who opted in, and a replay from the same game. Keep any pre-release exhibition explicitly labelled. Recheck the applicable competition rules before describing a public queue or ladder.
