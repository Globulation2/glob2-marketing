# Globulation 2 MCP outreach research

[Planning index](../README.md) · [Working launch positioning](../../analysis/positioning-v1.md) · [Mobile press activity](../mobile-press-outreach.md)

**Research completed November 23, 2026.** The dossiers contain 39 editorial leads and 23 creator leads, plus 10 ecosystem and collaboration routes. The outreach angle is a free, open-source colony strategy game where a player can share controls with a coding agent over Model Context Protocol (MCP), ask it to write one small colony-management routine, and keep playing alongside it. The project owner expects public-server deployment overnight November 23–24. The recommended launch story leads with human-plus-agent online competition once the rollout is visible. The local standing-order demo gives journalists and creators an accessible way to try the underlying idea. No outreach was sent for this project.

## What the research found

- **Use the playable workflow as the hook.** The strongest visual is a player asking a coding agent to write a standing order for a recurring job, then changing the colony's strategy manually while the routine runs. It builds on why Glob2 is interesting to play: food, labour and territory compete for the player's attention.
- **Drop the broad “first game playable through MCP” claim.** Minecraft bridges and the Orak StarCraft II integration precede Glob2. OpenRA-RL is another clear RTS precedent. Agent Command is direct overlap for human/agent co-play and publicly described MCP multiplayer. Glob2's established colony mechanics and opt-in desktop integration are a better story. [Product and precedent review](product-and-prior-art.md)
- **Give online competition its own proof.** A merged change adds separate MCP-assisted casual and ranked queues, but the latest checked public release does not include it and production lists only ordinary queues. For a nearer-term exhibition, test an invite-only match over the Internet with consenting humans and clearly identified UI-level MCP assistance. The local JavaScript standing-order workflow is separate. [Online evidence and claims](product-and-prior-art.md#glob2-capabilities-and-availability)
- **Use different approaches for different audiences.** AI and developer publications need a copyable prompt and enough detail to reproduce the experiment. RTS journalists need to see the colony decision and its consequences. Creators need a challenge with a clear objective, some room for interpretation and an honest result.

## How leads were evaluated

Three parallel research lanes searched official bylines, publisher policies, issue archives and creator-owned channels for relevant evidence. Priority favors direct coverage of agents playing games, recent hands-on work on MCP or coding agents, or a demonstrated audience for free RTS games and human-versus-bot challenges. A public tip or business route raises confidence when it fits that request. Public profiles, sponsorship forms, community channels and developer-submitted placements have different uses, which the dossiers identify. We kept multiple bylines at one newsroom as alternative routes and included promising leads with weaker evidence in the individual files.

The coverage is English-first with selected German and French leads. This is a targeted editorial map, with public routes and dated examples. Audience size, editorial interest, delivery and willingness to collaborate are unverified. Creator statistics, where available, are dated snapshots of individual items. The project links to public information and excludes private contact data. The desktop endpoint was independently smoke-tested on macOS. The full co-play demonstration, Internet exhibition and outreach remain open for a future execution pass.
