## Approved master pitch

**Build a thriving colony in contested territory.**

Globulation 2 is a real-time strategy game where food, workers and space shape your expansion. Grow a sprawling colony, balance its competing needs, and contend with rivals for land and resources. Your creatures act autonomously, letting you guide the colony as a whole.

## About — draft description blocks

Paste each headline and body into an Epic description block. The following text contains no store-availability, multiplayer-readiness or optional-service claims.

### Build a thriving colony in contested territory.

Food, workers and space shape your expansion in Globulation 2. Grow a sprawling colony, balance its competing needs, and contend with rivals for land and resources. Your creatures act autonomously, letting you guide the colony as a whole.

### Grow an economy that can sustain your ambitions

A growing colony needs more than buildings. Food, construction and training compete for your workforce. Decide where workers are needed, build the services your colony depends on, and balance new ambitions with the needs of the colony you already have.

### Make the map part of your strategy

Where you build matters. Shape your base around the land and resources, choose where to expand, and consider what each new foothold means for your economy and your rivals. Growth depends on both the means to expand and room to do it.

### Guide the colony as a whole

Set worker demand at buildings and place flags to direct activity. Your creatures find their own routes and attend to their needs. Guide the colony through its priorities, watch how your decisions affect the whole system, and adapt as its needs change.

### Defend what you've built

Rival colonies have ambitions of their own. Expansion brings competition for land and resources, and your economy must support both growth and defense. Build a colony that can sustain itself under pressure and prepare for conflicts that can decide the match.

### Free and open source

Globulation 2 is free and open source. Explore the game, inspect its source, and contribute to its continuing development.
